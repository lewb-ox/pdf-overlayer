# Editor direction

Mode: Operate. The user pinned a compact plan editor and direct implementation.

The mechanism is a translucent, closed room shape traced directly over a real PDF, assigned to an editable colour key and exported without rescaling the plan.

Grounded candidates, in resonance order: architectural drawing register; surveying fieldbook; planning committee drawing schedule; cartographic key; print preflight workbench; translucent tracing desk; facilities room register.

The assigned sixth direction is the translucent tracing desk. A warm neutral work surface supports white PDF paper, precise green controls, a quiet typographic signature in Newsreader and compact DM Sans controls. The PDF dominates; colour appears primarily in the user's work. Its risk is that paper warmth can obscure precision: retain crisp rules and legible control contrast.

The six catalog challengers were assessed against audience identification and task clarity. Type specimen, event catalog, dance notation, acetate manual and gate board were declined on both axes; a dark developer console was competitive on compact controls but weaker for viewing architectural paper. The tracing desk keeps the specimen's live feedback, the catalog's correspondence between list and spatial item, the console's isolated destructive actions, notation's explicit active state, the manual's crisp layering and the gate board's persistent row identity. No borrowed decorative motifs.

## First viewport
Full-height editor. A compact header carries Open PDF and Export PDF. The left column contains the editable colour key and room list; the central paper shows an explicitly labelled example vector floor plan with several translucent room overlays. A slim toolbar and page/zoom controls frame the drawing. The legend is a movable object on the actual PDF page. No marketing page precedes the task.

## Signature interaction
The open tracing path follows the pointer; its first vertex becomes a visible closure target. Straight and quadratic curve segments can alternate. Completed paths always close, and selecting a room reveals editable vertices and curve controls. Opacity and category changes appear live on both plan and legend.

## Adaptation
Desktop retains the colour panel. Smaller screens collapse it behind a labelled panel toggle; page and tools stay reachable. The canvas scrolls at intentional zoom rather than compressing drawing targets.
